ECHOES OF THE AWAKENING ISLE

A Top-Down Open World Action RPG — Aimed Attacks & Dodge Combat

🗡️ Sword & Aim

  • Mouse: Aim attacks & abilities
  • L-Click: 3-Hit sword slash combo
  • Hold L-Click: Charged 360° Spin Attack!

💨 Dodge & Defense

  • Space / Shift: Dodge Roll (i-frames!)
  • E: Shield Deflection (deflect rocks)
  • Dodge through shockwaves & charge attacks!

🏹 Bow & Items

  • R-Click / Q: Aim & shoot Bow
  • E: Lift & throw pots / Open chests
  • Tab / I: Inventory & Quest log

🗺️ Exploration

  • WASD: 8-Direction movement
  • Slash bushes & smash pots for Rupees
  • Explore Village, Wilds, Forest & Dungeon!
