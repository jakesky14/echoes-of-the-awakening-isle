ECHOES OF THE AWAKENING ISLE

A Top-Down Open World Action RPG — Aimed Attacks & Dodge Combat

🏃 Movement

  • W A S D or Arrow Keys
  • Smooth 8-direction movement
  • Works with QWERTY & AZERTY keyboards

🗡️ Sword & Aim

  • Mouse: Aim crosshair & attacks
  • L-Click or J: Sword slash combo
  • Hold L-Click / J: 360° Spin Attack!

💨 Dodge & Defense

  • Space / Shift / K: Dodge Roll (i-frames)
  • E / U: Shield (deflect enemy rocks)
  • Dodge through shockwaves & charges!

🏹 Items & Quests

  • R-Click / Q / L: Shoot Bow (aimed)
  • E / F: Throw pots & open chests
  • Tab / I / Esc: Inventory & Quests
